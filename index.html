      How a forecast is made

      Four stages run on every plot, refreshed as new passes and sensor readings arrive.

      Observe

      Sentinel-1 radar and Sentinel-2 optical passes from Copernicus, plus ISRO and INSAT products, give cloud-piercing soil wetness, vegetation vigour and rainfall estimates.

      Ground-truth

      Low-power soil probes report moisture, temperature and salinity. They calibrate the satellite signal so it holds at field scale.

      Forecast

      A model trained on historical monsoon records and reanalysis data predicts weekly rainfall and stress for each plot, with an uncertainty band.

      Advise

      Risks become dated actions: when to irrigate, delay sowing, drain, spray or harvest early, written for the crop and growth stage.
